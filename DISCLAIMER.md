## What the data is, and is not

All figures come from public federal and state sources, listed with access dates in
[PROVENANCE.md](PROVENANCE.md). Nothing here is original research, and nothing is a
prediction.

Three limits worth stating plainly:

**Outcomes are self-reported by training providers to the state, and by the state to the
federal government.** This project reproduces them; it does not verify them.

**A missing value means "not reported or withheld", never zero.** Under WIOA, results for
small groups are suppressed to protect the privacy of the people in them. Roughly a third of
California's programs report nothing at all. Absence of data is not evidence that a program
performs badly, and the interface is built to keep those two ideas apart.

**Occupation projections are the state's ten-year estimates, not guarantees.** A job the
state expects to grow may not. A job it expects to shrink may still be the right choice for
a particular person in a particular place.

## Not advice

Nothing here is financial, legal, educational, or career advice. Deciding to enrol in a
training program is a significant financial and personal commitment. Use this as one input,
and talk to the provider, to your local America's Job Center, or to a career counsellor
before deciding.

## Corrections

If a figure here misrepresents your program or organization, please open an issue. Where the
error is this project's, it will be fixed. Where the underlying public data is wrong, the
correction has to go through the body that published it, and the issue thread is a
reasonable place to record that.
